The lenient :func:`dateutil.parser.parse` now rejects numeric time zone
offsets whose hour component is outside 0-23 or whose minute component is
outside 0-59, matching the range already enforced by the strict
:class:`dateutil.parser.isoparser`. Previously, malformed offsets such as
``+25:00`` or ``+2360`` were parsed into a fixed offset that either raised a
:class:`ValueError` only later, when the resulting aware
:class:`datetime.datetime` was converted or serialized, or was silently
converted to a wrong offset. In addition, :class:`dateutil.tz.tzoffset`
now rejects offsets at or beyond +/- 24 hours at construction time, so both
parsing paths and the offset construction surface enforce the same boundary.
